# Story

The 1080 × 1920 Instagram and Telegram story. Same parts as `SocialPost`, set for a tall screen.

**Markup:** `<div class="sal-story sal-story--crimson|white|ink">` (add `dir="rtl" lang="ar"` for Arabic) wrapping `.sal-story__frame`, with `img.sal-post__lockup`, `h2.sal-post__headline`, `p.sal-post__lede` and `p.sal-post__meta`.

**Rules**
- Keep every word between 250 px from the top and 340 px from the bottom, clear of the app's own bars. The template does this.
- Leave the space under the sentence free for a link or poll sticker; the meta line names the link in words too.
- One language per story, as with posts.
