# Hudhud (هدهد), the Society's mascot

Hudhud is a hoopoe: the bird with the crown of feathers, native to Iraq,
that carries messages in the old stories and leads the birds on their
journey in Attar's *The Conference of the Birds*. For the AUIB Society of
Arts and Letters it is the messenger. It carries the Society's letter in
its beak: the SAL symbol, a sheet folded like a letter.

It is the face of the Telegram bot (`apps/web/lib/api/bot`) and may appear wherever
the Society speaks to members one to one: the bot, notices, empty states
that point somewhere. It is not a logo and never replaces one.

## Drawing

Flat shapes in the brand's own colors, nothing added:

| Part | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Body and head | crimson-100 `#ebcbd3`, outlined in ink `#273236` |
| Crest | crimson `#9c213e`, a white band, ink tips |
| Wings and tail | ink with white bars |
| Beak and legs | ink-70 `#4a5559` |
| The letter | white, outlined in ink |
| Avatar ground | crimson-50 `#f7ecef` |

No gradients, no shadows, no outlines but the 7 px ink line. Don't
recolor, stretch, mirror for a different direction of reading, or add
props, speech bubbles or expressions. Don't put it on ink or on a crimson
band: it is drawn for light grounds.

## Voice

Hudhud is warm, brief and literate, a little playful: a messenger who
knows the Society well. It says "I" and calls itself the Society's
messenger. It never shouts (no exclamation marks), never uses emoji, and
follows the copy rules in AGENTS.md (American English, "the Society" or
"SAL", dates as "Tuesday, October 13", times as "6:00 PM"). The Arabic
is written for the platform and needs a native review.

| Write | Not |
| --- | --- |
| "You're in. I'll remind you before it starts." | "Woohoo!! You're registered 🎉" |
| "Nothing on the calendar yet." | "Oops! No events found!" |
