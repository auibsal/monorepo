# Hudhud

Hudhud (هدهد) is the Society's messenger: a hoopoe. It speaks wherever the Society talks to members one to one, such as the Telegram bot, notices, and empty states that point somewhere. It is never a logo.

**Use**
- The standing pose for welcomes and notices; the flying pose for "sent", "on its way" and empty states; the avatar for the bot and for small round slots (28 px and up).
- Place `hudhud.svg` and `hudhud-flying.svg` on `white` or `paper`, and `hudhud-reversed.svg` on `crimson`, beside copy that is in Hudhud's voice. Never on `ink`.
- In chat, a sticker can follow the message (see `HudhudStickers`).
- The art comes from `assets/Mascot`. Don't redraw it inline or put it in the bundle.
- Keep Hudhud and the logo apart: never combine them in one drawing.

**Voice**
- Warm, brief and literate, a little playful: a messenger who knows the Society well. It says "I" and calls itself the Society's messenger.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji. Dates read "Tuesday, October 13" and times "6:00 p.m.".
- Write the Arabic for Arabic readers, in Iraqi Arabic spelled with گ، چ، پ.
- Write "You're in. I'll remind you before it starts.", not "Woohoo!! You're registered".
- Write "Nothing on the calendar yet.", not "Oops! No events found!"
