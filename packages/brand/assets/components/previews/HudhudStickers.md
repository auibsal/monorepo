# HudhudStickers

The twelve stickers of Hudhud's Telegram set, shown on a light chat and a dark chat background. The files are in `assets/Stickers`; see `assets/Stickers/README.md` for their meanings and the emoji each one is filed under.

**Use**
- Send a sticker after the bot's message, never in place of the information. "You're in. I'll remind you before it starts." then 02 (you're in) works; the sticker alone does not.
- One sticker per reply at most.
- Match the sticker to the message: 06 (sent) after a submission is received, 07 (sorry) with an error message, 05 (asleep) when there's nothing new, 12 (oops) with a deadline reminder.
- In the members' group, stickers are for members to use. The bot doesn't post them in the group unprompted.
