The Typewriter Tour

The rules

  • One page per person.
  • Six words. Punctuation doesn’t count.
  • Typed on the Society’s typewriter.
  • Keep it campus-appropriate.

Your page

  • Join on auibsal.org at the station.
  • You choose in your account who sees your page. It’s private until you choose.
  • You take your page home.

Typewriter tips

Press the keys hard.

For 1, type a lowercase l.

For !, type a period, backspace, then an apostrophe.
