# Posters

The Society's two posters, A2 (420 × 594 mm), each in an English and an Arabic edition. Each is an HTML page printed from the browser; `bun run --cwd packages/brand print` makes the PDFs into `dist/print`, which are sent to Student Life and never kept in the repository. Print only to the confirmed need, requested through the General Secretary's needs list.

| File | Poster | Sides |
|---|---|---|
| `typewriter-tour.html` | The Typewriter Tour | 2 |
| `typewriter-tour-ar.html` | The Typewriter Tour, Arabic | 2 |
| `society.html` | The Society | 1 |
| `society-ar.html` | The Society, Arabic | 1 |

**The Typewriter Tour** (crimson). The front: "Tell us a story. Six words only.", the photograph of Hemingway at his typewriter (`hemingway.webp`, a grayscale cutout on the crimson ground) and the quote "For sale: baby shoes, never worn.", attributed to Hemingway, set on the ground beside the photograph, never over it. The back: the lockup, the title, and four parts divided by hairlines: the rules (one page per person; six words, punctuation doesn't count; typed on the Society's typewriter; keep it campus-appropriate), your page (join on auibsal.org at the station; you choose in your account who sees your page, private until you choose; you take your page home), Join (the QR to https://auibsal.org/join) and who we are; then the typewriter tips. Nothing is signed, kept or archived, and there is no prize.

**The Society** (ink, for recruiting): the lockup, the motto "The paper and the pen." («والقرطاسُ والقلمُ»), Qirtas, our paper; the Company, our theater; events all year; and "Join at auibsal.org/join" with the QR. No personal photo, name, degree or email.

**Rules for every poster:** the Society's lockup in the poster's language, never AUIB's logo; one language per poster; headlines Light (Regular in Arabic) in one color; hairlines, never boxes; the QR in ink on a white square. The cover reads "SAL-MED-02 · <name>" in Ubuntu Mono at the top end.

**The photograph** is the one the old poster used, about 80 ppi at this size: a larger scan prints sharper. Confirm the rights to it before printing.
