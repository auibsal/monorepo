# Sticker pack

The Society's printed stickers: 37 die-cut stickers for laptops, bottles, notebooks and doors, printed by Student Life. Each file here is one sticker at 100%, its type outlined, with the cut line in magenta (`#ec008c`, 0.25 mm), which is cut and never printed.

- **Colors:** the six, with rose (`crimson-100`) as the accent on ink and in Hudhud. Never another color; the variety is in shape, type and structure.
- **Cuts:** die-cut stickers keep a 3 mm border (white vinyl, unprinted, or a solid crimson or ink border with 1.5 mm bleed); shaped stickers, the logos and the motto among them, are printed on a full ground and cut on its edge, with 1.5 mm bleed.
- **Hudhud** is used from the mascot files as supplied, never redrawn, and never touches the logo. The camera-shy sticker is the crossed-out camera alone.
- **Arabic** stickers (the motto, قرطاس, ق) need a native speaker's check before printing (needs-native-review).
- **Material:** white matte vinyl; "Six words, to fill in" on a writable matte stock.
- The printer gets a PDF made from these files (a cover with the specs, the overview, and one page per sticker at 100%); it is sent, never kept in the repository. The Print story "Sticker pack" shows them all.
