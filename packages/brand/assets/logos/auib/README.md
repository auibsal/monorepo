AUIB's standard logo set (decisions.md, The site: "Where files live"): the American University of Iraq – Baghdad and the campus offices the Society names as campus partners. The site serves an unchanged copy from `apps/web/public/institutions` (checked by `tokens.test.ts`). Use them exactly as supplied; never redraw or recolor them. Files without a suffix are for light grounds, `-white` for ink.

- `auib(-white).svg`: the American University of Iraq – Baghdad
- `cas(-white).svg`, `library(-white).svg`, `press(-white).svg`, `student-life(-white).svg`: campus offices
- `american-space-logo-color.png` (3832 × 600): the American Space's one lockup, the U.S. flag, the seal of the United States Mission Iraq, the American Spaces mark and AUIB's, in their own colors. There is no white version: in dark mode it sits on the white band like every logo in a row.

The footer's line "A registered student organization at the American University of Iraq – Baghdad" shows AUIB's logo once the official file from AUIB is placed here.
