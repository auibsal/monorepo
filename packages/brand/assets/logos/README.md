The Society's identity: the symbol, the two wordmarks and the lockups built from them. All of them are vector, with text outlined (Ubuntu Bold, in both languages), so no fonts are needed. Use them as `<img>`; their colors are fixed, so never recolor them.

**One language per piece.** Use the logo in the language of the content: the English lockup on English pages, posts and documents; the Arabic lockup on Arabic ones. Never both on one piece.

**Color (one hue).** Files without a suffix are for light grounds (`white`, `paper`): crimson `#9c213e` symbol, Crimson 700 `#7e1a32` wordmarks. `-reversed` files are for `crimson` and `ink` grounds: white symbol and white wordmarks. They are invisible on white.

**Lockups**
- `sal-lockup-horizontal(-reversed).svg`: symbol + English wordmark. For everything in English.
- `sal-lockup-horizontal-ar(-reversed).svg`: Arabic wordmark + symbol, the symbol on the right. For everything in Arabic.
- `sal-lockup-stacked(-reversed).svg`: symbol over the English wordmark. For square spaces, stickers and merchandise.
- `sal-lockup-bilingual(-reversed).svg`: English | symbol | Arabic. Only for things that must speak to both at once and stay up for a long time: permanent signage, banners and merchandise.

**Parts**
- `sal-symbol.svg` (crimson) and `sal-symbol-white.svg`: the symbol alone, for favicons, stamps and badges. The symbol is called the Bookmark (Arabic «العلامة»). It is only ever the logo: never a bullet, a section marker or a decoration.
- `sal-wordmark-en(-reversed).svg`: "AUIB / Society of / Arts and / Letters".
- `sal-wordmark-ar(-reversed).svg`: «جمعيةُ الفنونِ / والآدابِ في / الجامعةِ / الأمريكيةِ». The wordmark alone closes every carousel, in the carousel's language.
- `sal-avatar.svg`: white symbol on a crimson square: the profile picture for Instagram and the members' Telegram group.
- `sal-avatar-ink.svg`: white symbol on an ink square: the profile picture for the Diwan group, the officers' working group.

**Proportions.** In lockups the symbol is as tall as the English wordmark, from the first cap line to the last baseline. The gap between symbol and text equals the depth of the notch. Clear space is one notch depth on every side.

**Minimum sizes.** Symbol: 24 px tall on screen or 6 mm printed. Horizontal lockup: 45 mm wide in print. Below that, use the symbol alone.
