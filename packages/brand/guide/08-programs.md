# The family of programs

Six programs, listed by name and never grouped into tiers. Each may have its own title treatment; all of them share the Society's colors and type.

| Program | |
| --- | --- |
| Second Chapter | Every November: a used-book drive, then a fair. |
| Typewriter Tour | Every March, after Eid. Ubuntu Mono for the six-word lines, typed on the Society's machine. |
| The Salon | Tuesdays, every fourth week: a guest, three readings, tea and conversation. |
| Book Circle | Tuesdays, every fourth week: one book a month, read together. |
| Open Pages | Tuesdays, every fourth week: a writing workshop. |
| The Listening Room | Tuesdays, every fourth week: an album or recording played in full, then talked about. |

**Every program must**

- Use the six colors and the Ubuntu family. Never add a color.
- Carry the Society's lockup, or the line "a Society of Arts and Letters program" (in Arabic: «من برامج جمعية الفنون والآداب»), in the piece's language.
- Tag @auibsal on every post.

**A program may**

- Have its own name treatment, approved by the Director of Media.
- Use its own handle once it has a year of content.
- Use paper or Ubuntu Mono as its accent.
