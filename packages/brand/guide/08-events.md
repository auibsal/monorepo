# The family of events

Qirtas is the Society's paper and the Company its theater; everything else is an event (owner, Nov 5, 2026). The Society holds no standing programs. Each term the Council sets a calendar of events, at least one a month. Events carry no standing names (owner, Nov 6, 2026): each is titled plainly for what happens that day, and none is a program. The kinds below recur; all of them share the Society's colors and type.

| Kind of event | |
| --- | --- |
| Book drive and fair | Every November: a book drive, then a book fair, for a charity. |
| Readings and conversation | Tuesdays, as the term calendar sets: a guest, three readings, tea and conversation; sometimes a debate. |
| Book discussion | Tuesdays, as the term calendar sets: one book, read and discussed; sometimes a Friday morning on al-Mutanabbi Street. |
| Writing workshop | Tuesdays, as the term calendar sets: a writing workshop; sometimes a worst-poem contest. |
| Listening session | Tuesdays, as the term calendar sets: an album or recording played in full, then talked about. |

The **Typewriter Tour** is the Society's welcome to new students and belongs to membership (Join), not to the events: once a year in spring, after Eid, and the typewriter at the fall club fair. Ubuntu Mono for the six-word lines, typed on the Society's machine.

**Every event must**

- Use the six colors and the Ubuntu family. Never add a color.
- Carry the Society's lockup, or the line "a Society of Arts and Letters program" (in Arabic: «من برامج جمعية الفنون والآداب»), in the piece's language.
- Tag @auibsal on every post.

**An event may**

- Have its own name treatment, approved by the Director of Media.
- Use paper or Ubuntu Mono as its accent.
