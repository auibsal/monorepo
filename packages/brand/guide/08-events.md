# Events

Qirtas is the Society's paper, the Company its theater and Next Stop its video show; everything else is an event. The Society holds no standing programs and no recurring named kinds of event. Each semester the Council sets a calendar of events, at least one a month, each titled plainly for what happens that day: "Writing workshop", "A reading with a guest", "A trip to al-Mutanabbi Street".

## One standard event page

Every event, Council meetings, general meetings and the Edition's launch among them, uses the same page on Events (auibsal.org/events). Only the details change: the title, the date and time, the place, the description, one image, the lead and the links. No event has its own look, its own components or its own name treatment.

| Access | Who comes |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Anyone may come; no RSVP. |
| RSVP | Members and guests book a place, and the door checks them in. |
| Private | Invited people only, for example a Council meeting. |

Every event may carry the same standard parts, and no others: RSVP and check-in, a needs list, volunteer shifts, a partner checklist, and links from the night (photos, recordings, the Qirtas piece, minutes).

## The two traditions

An event earns a name only once it has run at least twice and the Council agrees. There are two:

- **The Typewriter Tour** (membership): once a year in the spring club fair week, with the fair as one of its days. Paperless from spring 2027: each writer joins with the QR join card, the typed page is photographed into their account, and the writer takes the original home. Ubuntu Mono for the six-word lines.
- **Second Chapter** (partnerships): a book drive and sale for a charity, at the fall club fair, from October 2027.

Both use the standard event page; their names are set like any title, in Light.

**Every event**

- Uses the six colors and the Ubuntu family. Never add a color.
- Carries the Society's lockup, or the line "a Society of Arts and Letters program" (in Arabic: «من برامج جمعية الفنون والآداب»), in the piece's language.
- Tags @auibsal on every post.
- Prints only to the confirmed need: the RSVP count plus 10%. Tickets, sign-ups and forms are on the site.
