# The logo

## The Bookmark

A crimson panel with a V-notch cut into its top. Turned over, it is the tail of a ribbon bookmark: the mark that keeps a reader's place. Its name is the Bookmark, in Arabic «العلامة».

![The Bookmark](brand/sal-symbol.svg)

- **Why a bookmark.** It marks a place in a book: the Society keeps yours. It reads as a page, a ribbon and the notch of the Typewriter's paper guide.
- **Only the logo.** The Bookmark appears once on a page, inside the lockup or as the avatar. It is never a bullet, a section marker, a ribbon, an icon or a decoration.
- **The rules.** Crimson on white or paper; white on crimson or ink (`sal-symbol-white.svg`). Never crimson on ink, never outlined, rotated in use, given a gradient or a second color.

## The wordmarks

The name in two languages, each on four lines, drawn once and never retyped.

- `sal-wordmark-en.svg`: "AUIB / Society of / Arts and / Letters" in Ubuntu Bold, left-aligned.
- `sal-wordmark-ar.svg`: «جمعيةُ الفنونِ / والآدابِ في / الجامعةِ / الأمريكيةِ» in Ubuntu Arabic Bold, right-aligned.
- Color is one hue: `crimson-700` wordmarks on light grounds; white on crimson and ink (the reversed files: white symbol and white wordmark).
- Text is outlined in the files, so no fonts are needed. Line breaks, spacing and proportions are fixed.
- Below about 9 px cap height the four lines stop being legible: use the Bookmark alone.
- The wordmark alone closes every carousel, English then Arabic.
- Never set the wordmark in ink, retype it, re-break its lines or set it on one line.

## Lockups

The symbol and a wordmark, in the language of the piece. Each comes in a light and a reversed file. The logo appears once per page or post.

| Lockup | For |
| --- | --- |
| English (`sal-lockup-horizontal`) | Everything in English: headers, covers, letterheads, posts |
| English, reversed | On ink and crimson |
| Arabic (`sal-lockup-horizontal-ar`) | Everything in Arabic, the symbol on the right |
| Arabic, reversed | On crimson and ink |
| Stacked (`sal-lockup-stacked`) | Square spaces, stickers, merchandise |
| The avatar and the symbol alone | Favicons, stamps, badges |
| Bilingual (`sal-lockup-bilingual`) | Only permanent signage, banners and merchandise that must speak to both languages at once |

**Built in.** In every lockup the symbol stands as tall as the English wordmark, from the first cap line to the last baseline, and the gap between them equals the depth of the notch. Never assemble a lockup by hand.

## Clear space and minimum sizes

Clear space is one notch depth (about a quarter of the symbol's width) on every side.

| Mark | Smallest |
| --- | --- |
| Bookmark | 16 px on screen, 5 mm printed |
| Horizontal lockup | 45 mm wide in print; 40 px tall on screen |
| Wordmark | 9 px cap height; below that, the Bookmark alone |
| Hudhud | 48 px; his avatar 28 px |

**With the University.** Where the AUIB logo appears it leads; the Society's lockup follows at equal or smaller height. Inside AUIB's own templates, follow AUIB.

## Never
