# Social and print

## Posts and print

One idea per post, in one language. The headline does the work; nothing sits above it. The files are in Social; the printables in Print.

**Posts (1080 × 1350)**

- English posts carry the English lockup; Arabic posts the Arabic lockup, mirrored. Post them separately.
- The lockup at the top start; two to five words with a full stop in Light, in one color; one sentence; the date or link and @auibsal at the bottom start.
- Grounds by purpose: crimson for calls and moments, white for events, ink for words worth keeping and recruiting.
- Photo posts: the picture above (1080 × 820), the words on a solid ink or white panel, the lockup at the bottom end. Never type over a photograph.
- Instagram crops the grid to 3:4: keep words 80 px from the side edges.

**Print**

Paperless by default: print only to the confirmed need (the RSVP count plus 10%, or the number actually asked for), and booklets in page counts of multiples of 4. Every printed piece is requested from Student Life through the General Secretary's needs list.

- Posters A3/A4: lockup, Light headline, a QR code at the bottom end.
- Forms, letters, membership cards and certificates are components, printed from their own pages (see Documents and forms). The printables keep only what exists only on paper: name badges, the photography notice and the sticker pack.
- Signage and banners may use the bilingual lockup.

## Carousels and stories

A carousel reads as one piece, in one language and on one ground. A story is a post set for a tall screen.

- **Cover:** a post, and the only slide with the logo.
- **Inside slides:** a large Light number ("01") in accent, a short title with a full stop, one to three lines.
- **The last slide:** the wordmark alone, in the carousel's language. No symbol, no text, no handle.
- No slide counters and no "swipe" prompts: Instagram shows the dots. Six slides or fewer; ten at most.
- **Stories, 1080 × 1920:** every word at least 250 px from the top and 340 px from the bottom, clear of the app's own bars; the space under the sentence is left for a link sticker.

Six highlights, each opened by a banner that says what is inside. Covers carry an icon alone; the banners do the explaining.

- **Covers:** plain crimson with the highlight's icon alone, in white, centered inside the middle 760 px of a 1080 × 1080 square. No name (Instagram writes it under the circle), no Bookmark, no other text.
- **Banners:** the first story in each highlight, 1080 × 1920 on white. The wordmark alone at the top (no Bookmark), a Light headline in ink, one short line, a list split by thin rules, and the link at the bottom.
