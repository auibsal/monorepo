# Qirtas

The Society's paper. Its name, «قرطاس», is the paper of our motto: AUIB's paper of news, letters and art, online all year, with one printed Edition a year. Qirtas also runs Qalam, its summer program for high school students.

The front page: the nameplate, the dateline between ink rules, the six sections, a lead story and a column of headlines. No empty image boxes: a story without a picture gets a typographic layout.

## The nameplate

One nameplate on every page of the paper, the same on the front page, a section and a piece.

- "Qirtas" in Ubuntu Medium (500), set tight, never Light, never in crimson. On Arabic pages, «قرطاس» in Ubuntu Bold.
- Under it, the dateline between two ink rules: the date, the line "AUIB's paper of news, letters and art", and links to the Masthead, the Editions and the Mutanabbi Prize.
- Then the six sections as a row of text links, always in this order. No logo inside the nameplate: the site header already carries it. The masthead is the paper's list of editors, at /qirtas/masthead, from the roles people hold.
- Qirtas pages sit on paper (`surface-reading`), the one place the page is not white.

## The six sections

| Section | What it carries |
| --- | --- |
| News | Reports, features, interviews, profiles, event coverage and explainers. |
| Voices | Reviews of books, films, plays and exhibitions; columns, opinion essays, letters to the editor, debates and recommendations. |
| Letters | Poetry, fiction, creative nonfiction, drama and translation. |
| Gallery | Art, photography, illustration, comics, calligraphy and photo essays. |
| Video | Members' films and video stories, recorded performances and animation. |
| Games | The word, the word search, the crossword and the anagrams, made anew every day in English and Arabic. Members send no games. |

Six Words and Next Stop are not part of Qirtas: six-word pages belong to the Typewriter Tour, and Next Stop is the Society's video show, made by the Director of Media's team.

**Write for Qirtas** (auibsal.org/qirtas/submit) is always open: members send work in every section but Games, at any time, and everything is read blind. **The Sunday Paper** is the members' newsletter, monthly, on the first Sunday.

## Reading Qirtas

Headlines in Ubuntu, reading in Markazi Text, in both languages. Every article page is built the same way, so the words do the work.

| Part | Set in |
| --- | --- |
| Headline | Ubuntu Light, display, in ink |
| Dek | Markazi Text 26 / 1.5 (`read-dek`) |
| Byline | The section in accent, then the author and date: "Letters · By … · Tuesday, November 17". Never above the headline. |
| Body | Markazi Text 21 / 1.6 (`read-body`), 60–70 characters a line; Arabic at 1.8 |
| Drop cap | Markazi Text Medium in accent, first paragraph only |
| Pull quote | Ubuntu Light 34 px, one color |

## The Edition

- **One printed Edition a year, a year in review:** the year's top News stories and the best of Voices, Letters and Gallery, a crossword, and Video pieces shown as a still with a QR code. On paper, with the nameplate on the cover and the lockup small at the foot.
- It is launched at an event, titled plainly like any other; its posts use the crimson ground.
- Print to the confirmed need, in page counts of multiples of 4. Printing is requested from Student Life.
- "Thank you." closes the printed Edition; the online pages end with their last piece.
- **Read blind:** every piece is read without its author's name. Arabic and English pieces run in their own editions of the page.

## The Mutanabbi Prize

One prize in two categories, Qirtas and Qalam, given at the Annual Meeting each April from 2028. Winners are featured in the Edition and receive a certificate (see Print). No other awards.
