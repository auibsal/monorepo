# Photography

Our own photos of our own people, taken with consent, always beat stock images.

| Shoot | Never shoot |
| --- | --- |
| Hands on typewriter keys, pages, books being handed over | Anyone wearing a camera-shy sticker |
| Readers mid-sentence; full rooms | Identifiable children, ever |
| Close-ups only with consent | People praying, in distress or in private moments |
| Natural light; no heavy filters | Stock photos of books or typewriters in place of ours |

| Treat | Keep |
| --- | --- |
| Color natural and slightly warm | Every event archived within a week in the Diwan |
| Duotone in crimson or ink for posters and covers | Consent log updated with every release (F-17) |
| Never set type over a photo: the words go on a solid panel | Two copies: the Diwan and one offline backup |
| No photo means a typographic layout: never an empty image box | |
