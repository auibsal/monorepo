# Photography

Our own photos of our own people, taken with consent, always beat stock images. The Director of Media's team covers events: each event's lead asks for coverage when planning, and the team adds the links to the event's page afterward ("From the night").

| Shoot | Never shoot |
| --- | --- |
| Hands on typewriter keys, pages, books being handed over | Anyone wearing a camera-shy sticker |
| Readers mid-sentence; full rooms | Identifiable children, ever |
| Close-ups only with consent | People praying, in distress or in private moments |
| Natural light; no heavy filters | Stock photos of books or typewriters in place of ours |

| Treat | Keep |
| --- | --- |
| Color natural and slightly warm | Each event's photos linked on its page within a week |
| Duotone in crimson or ink for posters and covers | Releases (F-15) signed on the site, at auibsal.org/forms/F-15; no photo log |
| Never set type over a photo: the words go on a solid panel | Members set their own photo choice in their account |
| No photo means a typographic layout: never an empty image box | Qalam: never filmed; photos only with a parent's consent (F-38), no faces on public channels |
