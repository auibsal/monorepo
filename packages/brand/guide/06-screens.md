# Screens

auibsal.org and the Diwan share one frame. Few boxes, big words, a lot of air. The English site is English only and the Arabic site Arabic only.

- **Header:** one line. The lockup in the page's language, five sections at most, the other language, the theme toggle and Sign in as text links. A hairline below.
- **Front door:** the motto, one sentence, one button. Section titles stand alone, with at most a link at the end of the line.
- **Events:** a dated list, large Light day numbers down the margin, like an almanac.
- **Qirtas:** the Society's paper, on paper, with a masthead and no empty image spaces.
- **"Come in."** One ink band with the invitation, then the ink footer: three short columns.
- **Arabic pages** mirror the layout, with the Arabic lockup and Ubuntu Regular for display.
- **Phones:** nothing scrolls sideways at 360 px. Errors say what happened and what to do; no exclamation marks.

## In Arabic, on phones

The Arabic site is its own edition: the same layout mirrored, the Arabic lockup, Ubuntu Regular. Phones get the same page in one column.

- **Mirrored:** `dir="rtl"` on the page; the lockup at the top right; links and dates on the start side.
- Display in Ubuntu Regular at 1.3 leading.
- Latin strings (@auibsal, links, emails) are wrapped in `<bdi dir="ltr">` so they read the right way.
- **Phones:** the lockup at 40 px, a Menu link in place of the sections, the motto at 56–64 px.
- Lists keep their dates; the time and place move under the title.
- Nothing scrolls sideways at 360 px. Tap targets are 44 px or larger.
