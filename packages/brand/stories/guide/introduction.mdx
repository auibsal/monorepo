# The brand kit

This is SAL-BRD-01, Brand and Identity, for the AUIB Society of Arts and
Letters: everything that carries the Society's name, in one place. The
site, the Diwan, every email and every post use the same tokens,
components and logos shown here: the apps import them from the packages
this kit is built from, so what you see is what ships.

The kit is read-only. To change anything, change it in the repository
(`packages/brand`) and it follows here and on the site.

## What is here

- **Guide:** the brand book, in full, and the full guide as a PDF
  ([download](brand-kit/sal-brd-01-brand-and-identity.pdf)).
- **Foundations:** the six colors and their roles, type, space and shape,
  and the icon set.
- **Logos:** the symbol, the wordmarks and the lockups, each with its
  download.
- **Components:** the brand components the site and the Diwan are built
  from, the screens, and Hudhud.
- **Emails:** every email the platform sends, in English and Arabic,
  rendered from the real templates.
- **Social:** the post templates, carousels, story banners, the six
  highlight covers and the profile pictures, as PNG downloads.
- **Print:** the Term Card, membership cards, certificates, name badges
  and notices, as a PDF to print.

## In short

- **Quiet by default.** White space, hairline rules and sentence case.
  One loud thing a page: a headline, one crimson word or one band.
- **One language per piece.** English or Arabic, with the logo in that
  language. Never both.
- **The page is the brand.** The Bookmark appears only as the logo.
  Headings stand alone. Icons carry meaning; they never decorate.
- **American English**, no emoji, no exclamation marks in headlines.
