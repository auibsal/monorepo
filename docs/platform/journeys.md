# Journey maps

One short map for each kind of person (decisions.md, Members), checked
once a year in September. Each step names where it happens.

## A student member
1. Joins at auibsal.org/join (or from a shared link or a QR at the fair):
   address and password; the legal name and the Arabic form; the
   agreements. Confirms the address from the email.
2. Today shows the next steps: the profile (preferred names, language),
   the card in Wallet, the Telegram link (Hudhud approves the join
   request). The first two weeks' emails each point to one step and stop
   once it is done ("Get involved" on day 3, "Your first event" on day 10).
3. RSVPs on Events, checks in at the door with the card, gets a thank-you
   with one question after the first check-in.
4. Writes for Qirtas, applies for a role on Get involved, votes once in
   good standing.
5. Each September: "I'm still here" and the Code of Conduct again.
6. Graduates or leaves: becomes an Associate on the same account and
   number.

## An Associate (faculty, staff, alumni)
1. "Request Associate membership" on /join, approved by the Director of
   Membership, or an invitation by email.
2. Comes to events and writes; no vote or office.

## A guest
1. Signs up as a guest, or accepts a single-use invitation (14 days).
2. Tabs: Today, Events, Work, Documents, Settings. RSVPs to open events,
   writes for Qirtas (not Games), with their name and a short line.

## A parent
1. Gets the consent link when their child under 18 signs up; consenting
   makes a guest account with the Parent role linked to the child.
2. Sees and withdraws the child's consents. The role ends when the child
   turns 18 or Qalam ends; the guest account stays.

## A partner's people
1. The Partner Lead is invited when the partnership is approved; the Lead
   invites the team.
2. The partner dashboard: Events, Checklist, Guests, Team, Memorandum.

## A new role holder
1. Accepts the offer in the account; signs the role agreement (the Code of
   Conduct and the license for work made in the role), then the
   conflict-of-interest declaration; for Qirtas, the confidentiality
   agreement.
2. Sets up two-step sign-in where the role needs it; Hudhud adds them to
   the Diwan group; the checklist (their manual, Brand essentials, their
   Diwan pages) sits in the account.
3. The appointer confirms them at the end of the first term.

## A leaving role holder
1. Site access ends the same day; the handover is signed; Telegram
   removal follows seven days later; a seat's mailbox password is reset.
2. A service letter is offered automatically.

## Student Life
1. An Associate account with the outside role "Student Life".
2. Answers requests and approves trips from the link in each request
   (which signs them in); sees Security's guest lists.
