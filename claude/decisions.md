## 1. What the Society does
- **Qirtas** is the Society's paper, with its own masthead and editorial independence; the President, as Publisher, never selects work. It includes **Qalam**, its summer program for high school students.
- **The Company** is the Society's theater company. Until an Artistic Director is appointed, the President covers the seat and runs Company events. Its performances are ordinary events.
- **Next Stop** is the Society's video show, made by the Director of Media's team (a producer and the host, Hassan Sadiq): ~30-minute episodes, longer specials and reels, a reality-sitcom about AUIB. It is not part of Qirtas, and it can become its own club if it outgrows the Society.
- **Events.** The Society holds no standing programs. Events are made as needed, by the Society or for any party within or outside it, titled plainly for what they are. Each term the Council sets a calendar of events, at least one a month; any event can be added, changed, moved or dropped. An event that recurs is made again for each date; there are no series.
- **Traditions.** An event gets a name only once it has run at least twice and the Council agrees, as American societies do with signature traditions. The two traditions are:
  - **The Typewriter Tour** (membership): once a year in the spring club fair week, with the fair as one of its days (dates follow Student Life's fair).
  - **Second Chapter** (partnerships): a book drive and sale for a charity, at the fall club fair, from October 2027.
- **Ways to run an event** are kept in the Events Manual (SAL-EVT-01) as plain formats: a reading with a guest, a book discussion, a writing workshop, a listening session, a debate, a trip, a performance.
- **Spring 2026 Tour prize (decided Nov 7):** canceled; no prize is awarded for the 2026 Tour.
- **Old drafts (Nov 7):** the December 2025 Founding Dossier, Constitution and Bylaws were never sent to Student Life; they are deleted, with no copy kept.
- **No paper at the Tour (decided Nov 7):** from spring 2027 there are no consent slips and no kept pages. At the station the writer joins on auibsal.org (a QR code), a trained volunteer with the site's per-shift "station" access photographs the typed page and attaches it to the new account by scanning the writer's live card, and the writer takes the original home. The writer chooses in their account who sees the page (private, their profile, or the Tour's page; private by default); that choice is the consent record. The Society never asks for a student ID number as a separate field: the AUIB email (which contains it) is the account's identifier, protected like all personal data and never shown publicly. Writers under 18 join, and their page stays private and the account limited until a parent signs the consent sent by link (§10, §11). Spring 2026: the pages are scanned and their consent is in the import file; once the import is done, the slips are shredded and the originals are offered back at the membership table, and unclaimed ones are shredded at the end of fall 2026.
- **Six Words.** Six-word stories are written only on the typewriter: at the Tour, or at the membership table when someone joins in person. They are not part of Qirtas and can't be submitted any other way. Officers record each page in the Diwan and attach it to the writer's member profile; pages appear on the Tour's page under Join only with the writer's consent.
- **Qalam (decided Nov 6).** Qirtas's summer program for high school students: workshops, mentoring by AUIB members, assignments and a certificate. Its sessions are ordinary events, so volunteers can help as well as staff. A Qalam lead runs it under the Editor-in-Chief; the Director of Partnerships finds the partner school. Safeguarding throughout: the school's and parents' written consent, two adults at all times, contact only through the teacher, no one-to-one meetings, written Student Life approval. High school students never use the Diwan or Get involved. With consent, the best work may appear in Qirtas, bylined with the student's first name, last initial and school; the full name appears only on the certificate. **Media and records (decided Nov 7):** Qalam is never filmed for Next Stop or any public video; photos only with a parent's consent (F-38), first names only, no faces on public channels; consent forms and attendance kept three years after the program, incident records seven years (or longer if AUIB's rules require), then shredded or deleted.
- **One events manager, one standard event.** Every event, including Council meetings, general meetings, the Tour's days, Second Chapter, Qalam sessions and the Edition's launch, is made in the events manager in Notion (the Events database) and copied to the site, where it is shown on What's on (auibsal.org/events) and takes RSVPs and check-in.
  - **Every event uses the same standard page;** only the details change: title, date and time, place, description, image, lead, and links.
  - **Access:** open (anyone may come, no RSVP), RSVP (book a place), or private (invited people only, for example a Council meeting).
  - **Standard parts only:** RSVP and check-in (attendance counts toward voting rights), a needs list, volunteer shifts, a partner checklist, and links from the night (photos, recordings, the Qirtas piece, minutes).
  - **Off campus (decided Nov 7):** every event has a standard "off campus" field; when it is set, the event can't be published until Student Life's written approval is recorded on it (date and who approved), and participants sign the trip consent form (F-45) on the site. Volunteer shifts exist only on an event and log no hours; there are no standalone rotas.
  - **No event-specific features anywhere:** nothing built for one event or one kind of event, on the public site, in the Diwan, on the partner dashboard or in guests' views.
  - **Photos and recordings:** the Director of Media's team covers events (with photography consent) and adds the links; each event's lead requests coverage when planning.
  - **Minutes and resolutions** are kept as records linked to their meeting's event.
- **Get involved** lists open roles only: Council and team roles, event teams, Qalam mentors and auditions. People apply in one step. Volunteer shifts exist only as an event's shifts.
- **The first term.** Everyone newly joining a team serves their first term on probation, which is also their trial; the person who appointed them then confirms them. Directors have no probation: the Executive Board approves them at appointment, after Student Life confirms eligibility (§10). Elected seats have no first term.

## 2. Members
- **Membership is open to every AUIB student,** with no discrimination and no dues.
- **Classes (decided Nov 6):**
  - **Members:** AUIB students who have joined on auibsal.org. Members in good standing follow the Code of Conduct. Voting members have attended at least two events or general meetings in the 12 months before the voter list's record date (§4).
  - **Associate Members:** AUIB faculty, staff and alumni; no vote; can't hold office.
  - **Honorary Members:** voted by the Council for outstanding contribution; no vote.
  - Partners and guests aren't members: partners are organizations in the partner register; guests attend open events and may create a guest account to RSVP, or are invited by a partner or by an event's lead; every guest has a number and a Guest card (decided Nov 7).
  - **Joining by class (decided Nov 7, the American standard), each class with its own welcome, Today and permissions in both languages:** students join themselves at auibsal.org/join (anyone may share the link) and confirm they are AUIB students; graduates move to Associate Member automatically at graduation, on their confirmed personal email, keeping the same account and number (a notice and a one-click confirmation); faculty and staff ask on /join ("Request Associate membership", approved by the Director of Membership) or receive an invitation; Honorary Members join after the Council's vote, by invitation with F-34. Invitations follow the one invitation system (§10). No link or invitation ever grants a role or a seat: roles are given only after an application or election and an appointment on record, by an officer on the member's page. Student Members vote, hold office, write and are eligible for the Prize; Associate and Honorary Members don't vote or hold office, but write and come to events; guests RSVP, write (bylined as guests) and aren't eligible for the Prize.
- **Partners (decided Nov 7, the American standard):**
  - **They apply; the Society never adds them.** An organization becomes a partner only by applying at auibsal.org/partners/apply (its profile, its contact and the partnership memorandum, F-17, signed on the site). The Director of Partnerships may send the link to an organization; the Council votes on each application, and only then is it a partner. Ending a partnership is a Council vote or the partner's own notice. Other AUIB clubs, charities and partner schools apply the same way. AUIB's own offices (AUIB, the Office of Student Life, AUIB Press, the Library, colleges) aren't partners: they appear as a fixed "With the support of" list on About and in the footer, kept in the site's settings and added by the web team on the Council's word. /partners lists Council-approved partners publicly (name, logo, one line, link) with their consent. A partner's edits to its listing wait for the Director of Partnerships' approval, and its name is locked to the signed memorandum.
  - **The partner's people (decided Nov 7):** each partner has a Partner Lead (the primary contact, who signed F-17) and up to two more team members, invited by the Lead by named email invitation. They see only their own events, checklist and guests on the partner dashboard.
  - **The partner's guests:** a partner invites guests to its events by named email invitation only (no shareable links); guests' names reach Security through Student Life automatically from the event's guest list two weeks ahead.
  - **No perks or offers; sponsors acknowledged (decided Nov 7, the IRS line between a sponsor acknowledgment and advertising):** partners don't list discounts, codes, offers or benefits for members anywhere. Sponsors in kind are thanked with a "Supported by" line (name or logo) on the event page, the partner's listing, printed programs and the thank-you (F-30), with no qualitative or comparative words, prices or calls to buy. The membership card is for the door and for checking membership, nothing else.
- **Joining:** on auibsal.org from launch day (Sunday, November 8). The spring 2026 Tour writers got one invitation, sent from the site on Saturday, November 7, 2026 (168 delivered, 13 bounced). The fall 2026 club fair sign-ups get the same invitation as soon as their paper sheets are logged on the site (decided Nov 7); the sheets are then shredded. Each invitation list is deleted thirty days after its send (the Tour list on December 7). There is no separate interest list.
- **The Register** is private, seen only by the Executive Board and the Director of Membership. Members may choose to appear in a members-only directory.
- **Records and certificates (decided Nov 6):** the Society records staff hours and service (the Council, the Qirtas masthead, team members) and writes a service letter on request. Student Life's own logbooks record event volunteers and professional-development trainees. Certificates are given only for finishing Qalam and for the Mutanabbi Prize; none for officers, staff or members.

## 3. Money: the Society has none (decided Oct 29)
- **The Society holds no budget, no account and no money, charges no dues, and has no Treasurer.** The Office of Student Life provides what the Society needs, through Ali Najm, Campus Recreation Officer, to the best of its ability.
- **Needs, not numbers.** Every event, the Company, Next Stop, the Typewriter Tour, Second Chapter, Qirtas, Qalam and the Council keep a **needs list**: the item, the quantity, the date it is needed, and what it is for. No document, page or form shows budgets, costs, prices, spending limits, ledgers, expense claims or reimbursements.
- **Who asks:** the Director of Events gathers each event's needs; the Director of Membership the Tour's; the Director of Partnerships Second Chapter's; the Editor-in-Chief the paper's and Qalam's; the Director of Media Next Stop's; the General Secretary sends every request to Student Life and logs what was asked, provided and when.
- **Plan around the answer.** Anything not provided is dropped or found through a partner (with the Council's approval).
- **No advertising and no paid sponsorship.** No ad networks, no paid ads, and no partner perks or offers. Sponsors give in kind (books, printing, a venue, refreshments), never money; each gift is a row in Needs and the sponsor is thanked by name. Offers of money are declined or referred to Student Life.
- **The one exception: charity collections** (Second Chapter and any other). The money belongs to the charity, never to the Society, and is never used for costs. The procedure is in the Policy Manual; each collection's record is a Notion page kept by the General Secretary, not a site feature.
  - Counted by two people at the close of each day, recorded, and kept sealed.
  - Handed over, witnessed, to the charity, with a receipt, and reported in an impact report.
  - The event's lead owns it; the General Secretary witnesses the counts and keeps the record. Cashiers are the event's volunteers.
  - "Every dinar goes to the charity."
  - Student Life advises how collected cash is held overnight and handed over.

## 4. Governance (the American standard, decided Nov 6)
### The Council: nine seats
- **The Executive Board:** the three officers, President, Vice President and General Secretary, elected by members. The President chairs it and the Council.
- **Six directors:** the Editor-in-Chief (Qirtas and Qalam), the Director of Media (promotion, design, channels, the web team, Next Stop), the Artistic Director (the Company), the Director of Events, the Director of Partnerships (partners, charities, Second Chapter) and the Director of Membership (the Register, the welcome, the Typewriter Tour, hiring, staff hours).
- **Covering open seats:** until each is filled, the President covers Events and the Artistic Director; the Vice President covers Partnerships; the General Secretary covers Membership.
- **The General Secretary:** records, minutes, the books, rooms, and every request to Student Life.
- No Treasurer. Central functions stay central: anything the Society does centrally is done by its owner, never by an event's or Qirtas's own team. Media promotes Qirtas but never decides its content.

### Elections and appointments
- **Officers:** elected by members by secret ballot.
- **The Editor-in-Chief:** elected by Qirtas's staff (editors and contributors with at least one term on Qirtas). The Editor-in-Chief heads Qirtas and Qalam only.
- **The other five directors:** appointed by the President with the Executive Board's approval, after an open call on Get involved; each serves until a successor is appointed (decided Nov 7). Eligible: a Student Member in good standing, enrolled, with a GPA of at least 2.0 (checked by Student Life); from fall 2028 also a member for at least one term.
- **Accounts (decided Nov 7, NIST SP 800-63B and OWASP ASVS):** passwords of at least 12 characters with no composition rules, a show-password toggle and a check against breached passwords; passkeys and two-step sign-in offered to everyone and required for Council roles (a passkey counts as two-step); the site never reveals whether an address has an account; sign-up asks the person to acknowledge the Code of Conduct, agree to the Terms and the Privacy Notice, and confirm they are an enrolled AUIB student or 18 or older (under-18 students: §10, Minors); one account per person, holding one class and any roles; every member can download and delete their own data (deletion keeps the name, roles held and published bylines).
- **Who can stand:** members in good standing, enrolled at AUIB, members for at least one term, with a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0. **GPA checks (decided Nov 7, the American standard):** always through Student Life, never by asking students for grades. Every candidate consents to the check on the nomination form; the Elections Committee sends the candidate list to Student Life before the ballot opens. Appointed directors are checked the same way before confirmation, and the General Secretary sends the full Council list to Student Life at the start of each term; anyone who falls below 2.0 is told privately and has one term to recover before the seat is declared vacant. Student Life answers only "eligible" or "not eligible"; the Society never sees or stores grades.
- **Who can vote (decided Nov 7):** voting members: at least two events or general meetings in the 12 months before the voter list's record date, for every election.
- **How votes are cast:** online on auibsal.org by secret ballot over a set window, with a voting point in person at the meeting. **Plurality (decided Nov 7):** one vote per seat; a candidate needs a majority of the votes cast, otherwise a runoff between the top two follows within a week (Robert's Rules). Members may vote online on any members' vote; the Bylaws authorize electronic voting.
- **First elections: October 2027.** The founding team serves until then; the first elected Council serves until May 2028; from 2028, elections are each April, with results at the Annual Meeting.
- **Vacancies:** if the President leaves office, the Vice President becomes President; another officer seat goes to a special election within 30 days; a director seat is filled the way it was first filled.
- **Term:** one year, from the new Council's first meeting in May; outgoing holders hand over in writing.
- **Removal:** a two-thirds vote of the Council (or of a members' meeting for an officer), after notice and a hearing.
- **Other votes:** amendments by members (two-thirds); Honorary Members, partners and removals by the Council; appointments approved by the Executive Board.

### Meetings and rules
- **Council meetings:** monthly in term; private events with minutes linked.
- **General meetings:** all members, every two weeks in term (news, plans, ideas, questions); they count toward the two-event voting rule.
- **The Annual Meeting:** each April from 2028 (election results, reports, the Mutanabbi Prize).
- **Special meetings:** called by the Council or by members, with two weeks' notice.
- **The Founding Meeting:** Tue Mar 30, 2027, 2:30–4:00 p.m. (decided Nov 7). Every Member in good standing who joined by the notice date (Tue Mar 16, two weeks before) may vote, in person or online; the Constitution and Bylaws are adopted by two-thirds of the votes cast, with the members' quorum (10% of those Members or 15, whichever is lower). The two-event rule applies from the first elections.
- **Rules of order:** Robert's Rules of Order, Newly Revised, where the Constitution and Bylaws are silent.
- **Amendments:** a two-thirds vote at a members' meeting (or online), with two weeks' written notice of the text.
- **Quorum:** the Council, a majority of the seats filled; a members' meeting, 10% of voting members or 15, whichever is lower.

## 5. Qirtas
### Six sections
| Section | What it takes (examples, not a fixed list) |
|---|---|
| News | Reports, features, interviews, profiles, event coverage, explainers |
| Voices | Reviews, columns, opinion essays, letters to the editor, debates, recommendations |
| Letters | Poetry, fiction, creative nonfiction, drama, translation |
| Gallery | Art, photography, illustration, comics, calligraphy, photo essays |
| Video | Members' films and video stories, recorded performances, animation |
| Games | The word, word search, crossword, anagrams |

- **Types are flexible:** each section takes what fits it.
- **Voices** keeps the rule for opinion: no party politics, religion, sect or ethnicity.
- **Video** publishes members' films and video stories (like a paper's multimedia desk); the Company may submit recordings of its performances.
- **Games:** daily, in English and Arabic, generated by the site and checked by the editors, numbered from No. 1 on the day they start; until the site generates them, there are no daily games. Members send no games.
- **One nameplate and a masthead** (auibsal.org/qirtas/masthead), from the roles people hold.

### How it comes out
- **Online, as pieces are ready,** from the site's launch.
- **One printed Edition a year, a year in review:** the year's top News stories and the best of Voices, Letters and Gallery, a crossword, and Video pieces shown as a still with a QR code; launched at an event; printing requested from Student Life.
- **The first Edition (decided Nov 7):** spring 2028, covering the site's launch to March 2028, with the first Mutanabbi Prize. Print specification: A5, about 80 pages (a multiple of 4), 100 gsm text, 8 color pages for Gallery, a 300 gsm cover, perfect bound; the run is the confirmed need.
- **The Sunday Paper (decided Nov 7):** the one newsletter, monthly on the first Sunday from the first Sunday after launch, put together and sent by the Managing Editor from the Diwan after a preview: Qirtas's pieces, then a labeled "From the Society" section and What's on. Members are subscribed at sign-up with a clear line and an opt-out box; the public subscribes at /sunday-paper with double opt-in; every issue has one-click unsubscribe and a no-login preferences page; sent issues are archived publicly. Its sign-up never asks a signed-in member to subscribe.
- **Write for Qirtas** (auibsal.org/qirtas/write) is the one door: members send work in every section except Games, at any time; everything is read blind. **Bylines (decided Nov 7):** the writer's name as in the Register, a pen name, or "Anonymous"; for a pen name or Anonymous the Editor-in-Chief must know the writer, and the identity is kept confidential.

### The Mutanabbi Prize (decided Nov 6)
- **One prize, two categories:** **Qirtas** (the year's best work published in Qirtas by an AUIB member) and **Qalam** (the year's best work by a Qalam student).
- **Given at the Annual Meeting each April, from 2028,** for the year to March; winners featured in the Edition.
- **Judging (decided Nov 7, one standard scoring for both categories):** editors draw up a shortlist in each category; one outside judge scores each shortlisted work out of 100 on the score sheet, reading blind; voting members vote online for one work in each category (Qalam students' names hidden; members only, never the public). Each work's vote share is scaled so the most-voted work scores 100. Final score = 70% judge + 30% members; a tie goes to the judge's score. The Editor-in-Chief and the General Secretary check the count.
- **Eligibility:** the Qirtas category is for student Members only (not Associate or Honorary Members), for work published in Qirtas from April 1 to March 31; the first Prize (April 2028) covers work from the site's launch to March 31, 2028. Pieces published anonymously or under a pen name are eligible when the Editor-in-Chief knows the writer; a winner chooses whether to be named. The Qalam category is first given in April 2029 (Qalam first runs in summer 2028). Editors, the judge and Council members recuse themselves from anything they are connected to.
- **Rewards:** a certificate; no cash.
- **Retired:** the Qirtas Awards, the other five awards and the chapbook.
- **Also retired (decided Nov 7):** calls for the Edition (Write for Qirtas is one door, any time; the Edition draws from published work), hand-made puzzles (the games are generated daily and checked by the editors in a queue), the optional member "insights" details, and any role not in the nine seats or the teams in the manuals.

## 10. Decisions of Nov 7, evening (the full audit; the American standard throughout)
- **One invitation system.** Every invitation that gives access (Associate or Honorary membership, a partner's team, a guest) is a single-use email invitation to a named person, valid 14 days, revocable, logged and accepted only with that address. They are sent by the Director of Membership (classes), a Partner Lead (the team, the partner's guests) or an event's lead (guests for that event). There are no shareable links and no Society-wide guest invitations.
- **Guests.** They sign up themselves or by invitation, have a number from the one series and a Guest card for check-in, and may write for Qirtas in any section but Games, bylined with their name and a short line saying who they are; they don't vote, hold office or compete for the Prize. Guests are 18 or older.
- **Minors.** Any enrolled AUIB student may join regardless of age. Members under 18 give a parent's or guardian's consent once (a form the parent signs electronically through a single-use link) and are covered by safeguarding: no one-on-one meetings with adults, consent for trips and photos. They may hold team roles but not Council seats until 18. Sign-up asks "Are you 18 or older?"; if not, the month and year of birth, used only to lift these rules at 18 and then deleted. No birthdays are collected otherwise, shown or celebrated. Qalam stays a separate program.
- **Names (legal and preferred, as US universities keep them).** A legal name, as on official papers, is required for Members in English and Arabic, entered in parts (given name, father's name, grandfather's name, and family name where there is one) and used only on certificates, letters, signed forms, minutes and lists for Student Life. A preferred name (first and last, chosen by the person) is shown everywhere else: the card, profile, bylines, the Council page and the masthead. Guests and partners' people give a preferred name, plus their full name when they sign something. Names are never required to be unique. Documents name seats only; holders appear on the Council page and masthead.
- **Electronic signatures (E-SIGN and UETA).** One standard wherever a signature is used (forms, memoranda, agreements, consents, nominations): the signer is signed in (parents and outsiders open a single-use link sent to their email), agrees to sign electronically, types their full name, which must match the legal name on the account, and confirms "I agree". The site records the name, number, time, IP address, the exact document version and its SHA-256 fingerprint, shows a signature block, emails the signer a copy and makes the record checkable at /verify.
