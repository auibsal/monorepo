## 2026-08-08 - Brutalist Focus Rings
**Learning:** In Brutalist interfaces with stark solid color blocks and sharp shadows, standard browser focus rings are often invisible or clash with the design.
**Action:** Always apply explicit high-contrast focus rings (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-primary focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-background`) to interactive elements to preserve keyboard accessibility without compromising the aesthetic.
