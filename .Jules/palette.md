## $(date +%Y-%m-%d) - Custom Button Focus States
**Learning:** Custom styled buttons throughout the `apps/nexus` application often lack `focus-visible` utility classes, making keyboard navigation difficult to track visually.
**Action:** Always ensure explicitly styled interactive elements include `focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-[color] focus-visible:ring-offset-2` to maintain accessibility standards.
