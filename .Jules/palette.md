## 2024-05-29 - Copy-Pasted Form Labels Breaking Accessibility
**Learning:** I discovered a pattern in Nexus where `<label htmlFor="key">` attributes were copy-pasted across multiple form inputs (e.g., `htmlFor="slug"` used for 'Volume', 'Issue', 'Title', and 'PDF' inputs; `htmlFor="tech"` used for multiple distinct rubric dropdowns). Additionally, the corresponding `id` attributes on the input elements were entirely missing. This severely breaks accessibility, as screen readers cannot associate the correct label with the input, and it breaks the native UX feature where clicking a label focuses its input.
**Action:** When working on or reviewing React forms in this application, specifically verify that every `<label>` has a unique `htmlFor` value that correctly matches the `id` of its intended input element, and utilize `<fieldset>` with `aria-labelledby` for groups of radio buttons instead of assigning a single `<label>` to wrap a container.
