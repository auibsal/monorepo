## 2024-05-18 - Missing Explicit Form Bindings and Required Field Indicators in Custom CMS Components
**Learning:** React form elements in the internal portal applications (like editorial CMS tools for events) were relying purely on physical proximity of `<label>` tags and inputs rather than explicit accessibility bindings. Moreover, required fields lacked visual indicators (`*`), preventing screen readers from accurately associating labels with inputs and confusing users about required form steps. This pattern was identified across multiple CMS files.
**Action:** Always ensure that `<label>` elements use the `htmlFor` attribute strongly bound to a unique `id` on the corresponding `<input>` or `<textarea>`, even when visually adjacent. Consistently append `<span className="text-auib-red">*</span>` or similar required indications to the label content for `required` fields across all portal forms.
